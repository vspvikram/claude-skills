Checkout system map

Today, checkout reserves stock and emails the receipt while the shopper waits. In this proposal the shopper waits only for the card charge: checkout saves the order and leaves a job in a queue, and a background worker does the rest. The order page reads what the worker writes, so no screen waits on the slow parts.

    Stores

    NameChange
    ordersAdds review_asked_at. Status moves paid → packing → shipped → delivered, or refunded.
    job_queue newid, kind (fulfil, review_email), payload, run_after, attempts, locked_until. A worker holds a job for 60 s.

    Rules

    • Checkout: the card call times out after 10 s, with no retry while the shopper waits.
    • Worker: up to 3 attempts per job, 1, 5 and 30 minutes apart, then support is told.
    • Review request: daily at 09:00 UTC. Skips refunded orders and shoppers who opted out.
